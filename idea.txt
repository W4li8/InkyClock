InkyClock - future ideas / nice-to-haves (not scheduled, just parking lot)

- Temperature sensor (e.g. a cheap I2C one like a BME280/SHT31) to auto-control
  the A/C, and auto-control blinds based on time of day. Would need a relay/
  IR-blaster or similar actuator for the A/C side and a motor or existing
  smart-blinds API for the blinds side -- bigger scope than the clock itself,
  parking here for later.

- Battery-backed external RTC module (DS3231 / PCF8523, I2C, coin-cell).
  The Pico has no battery-backed RTC (see conversation) -- it resets on
  every power loss and only gets corrected again once wifi.TimeSync
  succeeds. An external RTC would survive outages and give a sane baseline
  immediately at boot even before/without a successful Wi-Fi sync.
  Prefer PCF8523 over DS3231 -- lower backup current, and most PCF8523
  breakout boards skip the trickle-charge circuit that some cheap DS3231
  boards wire in for a rechargeable cell (which degrades/damages a normal
  non-rechargeable CR2032 if left in place -- check for it either way).
  Whichever chip: expect to swap the CR2032 every 5-10 years -- that's the
  battery's own shelf life, not something the RTC's ~1-3uA draw limits.

- Snooze button. Right now any press during MODE_RINGING just dismisses
  (alarm.stop_ringing() in main.py); there's no separate "quiet for N
  minutes then ring again" path.

- Display contrast/orientation flip. Useful if the case ends up mounted
  upside-down or at an angle; picographics likely has a rotate/flip call --
  check the picographics reference in docs/.

- Weekday-only / weekend-skip alarm toggle. alarm.py's Alarm already has an
  enabled flag (long-press C) -- this would extend that to a per-weekday
  mask instead of a single on/off.

- Debounce hardening. main.py's poll_button() is simple edge-polling, fine
  for the tactile switches on the Inky Pack, but worth a real debounce
  (e.g. requiring N consecutive stable reads) if double-triggers ever show
  up in practice.
